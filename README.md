# Puma Hackathon Research

Open research into Taipei civic problems: evidence, existing services, data limitations and
bounded experiments. The starting point is [市政 debug松](https://puma.taipei/hackathon), an event
organized by 沈伯洋競選辦公室. This project is independently maintained and has no organizer affiliation.

The first public edition covers **ten research tracks and 25 distinct workflows**. It contains
original research notes, cited source inventories and reproducible descriptive calculations.
Evidence retrieval is dated **2026-10-13**; source observation periods vary. No application,
registration, practitioner task test, adoption commitment or causal civic benefit is established.

## How to read the findings

The method starts with pain and the existing process, defines bounded success/rejection criteria,
then works backward from a user action to necessary evidence. Observations, inferences, hypotheses
and project decisions remain distinct. A readable endpoint is not proof of reusable or suitable
information; a working demonstration would not itself establish decision or civic benefit.
Recheck the original publisher before relying on changing event rules, service status or live data.

## Known limitations and review follow-ups

This is a research baseline, not a validated product proposal or an official operational guide.
The initial independent review identified three integration follow-ups:

- Newer YouBike availability categories and aggregate OD sources are documented in the source
  ledger; their implications deserve fuller cross-topic synthesis.
- Existing-tool comparisons often establish feature overlap, not observed user performance.
  Preserve potential comprehension/accessibility gains as untested hypotheses.
- The suggested two-school publication check is a bounded research experiment, not a winning
  topic. Compare which decisions different follow-up experiments would change, alongside effort
  and evidence acquisition constraints, before choosing a project.

The immature HTML presentation, internal agent/session records and local configuration are not
part of this public edition. Its history begins with a reviewed publication snapshot; internal
working history is not included. Earlier source observations remain dated within the brief.

## AI and data-source provenance

Research used GPT-6.1-Sol topic investigations, bounded GPT-6-Luna discovery and independent
GPT-6-Astra/Opus method consultation. AI output is a research aid, not source evidence. The brief
records original-publisher methods, retrieval dates, contrary evidence and unresolved questions.

Discovery used Twinkle Hub as one route alongside government portals, original researchers,
community organizations, operators, public websites/downloads and geospatial sources. Twinkle's
normalized or cached records do not replace original-source checks. No MCP configuration,
API keys, OAuth credentials or account/session exports are included. Reading this repository
requires no MCP account or authentication.

## Data and reuse

This repository publishes source links, original analysis and aggregate findings. It does not
redistribute raw datasets, citizen reports, personal records, downloaded papers, images or
restricted operator tables. Exact third-party terms remain in the source ledgers; some are
unknown or prohibit redistribution. Public access does not grant reuse permission. Reproduction
may require fetching the publisher's current files and complying with their terms; changed bytes
or publication periods must be recorded as new observations.
