### Professional practice and model boundaries

**Evidence — K01/K02 and E sources:** the applicable 危老 assessment method uses site inspection,
qualified institutions, structural-system-specific observations and checked reports. F quick-screen
output, initial-assessment R and other grade-score instruments have different meanings. In Annex5's
R branch, 甲 is R≤30 and 乙 is 30<R≤45; exactly 45 remains 乙. A professional structural result is
not a municipal allocation score or a demolition order. K01 Art.3 permits an owner-majority route
or an applicable committee submission of a unit-owner meeting resolution; this is not a universal
rule for every inspection programme. Preserve purpose, version, method, scope and date.

**Inference — indicator roles:** earthquake/site demand, structural vulnerability, exposed people,
rescue constraints and public-policy objectives must be represented separately before aggregation.
Liquefaction is only one site mechanism; it does not replace earthquake shaking/demand or a
local professional hazard basis. Without such a basis, do not describe the composite as absolute
seismic risk or predicted loss.
Older residents affect consequences/support needs, not concrete strength; fire access affects
rescue, not frame capacity; liquefaction susceptibility is a site clue requiring appropriate scale
and foundations, not a measured building loss probability. Construction/design/use-permit dates
must not be interchanged. Code-era and structure labels are incomplete vulnerability proxies;
soft-storey, alterations, details and material state need additional evidence. Known professional
hazard findings and urgent statutory actions require their own handling, not compensation by a
low population or convenient-consent score.

**Evidence — professional sensitivity:** the historic E10 teaching case moves R from 10.8 to
42.3 under two engineering assumptions. The strict D02/D03 subset is 99.30% recorded RC; it offers
little material-label contrast. Neither result calibrates municipal weights. FEMA rapid visual
screening is a professional precedent for preliminary triage and further assessment, not a
transferable Taipei coefficient table; the method audit in §5 gives scope and source pages.

why priorities differ without claiming the existing city process is deficient.

## 4. Candidate workflows

### Core model — two municipal decisions and one evidence chain

**Research design / hypothesis:** develop a transparent weighted seismic-risk evaluation model
with purpose-specific outputs A and B. The simplest shape is a shared, versioned evidence registry
and indicator dictionary, then separate decision rules and weight profiles for each action. The
registry is a research specification here, not a new system or implemented service.

Common source evidence → unit/time/scope checks → eligibility/known-condition routing →
indicator contributions and explicit weights → uncertainty/sensitivity → reviewer action and reason.
A feeds inspection/survey priority; B uses an appropriately aggregated risk/need profile to inform
project-area assessment together with separate suitability/feasibility evidence. A high A result
is not automatic B selection, demolition, grant approval or construction readiness.

### Indicator dictionary and weight provenance

**Structural-output → A connection (current building method):** for each located, non-superseded old new-build permit record, `E = p × O × (1 + δR) × (1 + λL)`. Each of the record's buildings inherits its fields. `p` is the class prior under the code design scenario; age/material/floors already determine it and receive no additional points. `O` is the 65+ share of the smallest statistical area, with the companion's declared small-population fallback. `R` records a matched listed lane or fire-access stretch; `L` is doorplate liquefaction potential (high 1, medium 0.5, low 0).

Four implementation readings × 100 era draws × nine δ/λ pairs produce 3,600 relative values. Each is normalized by the building-weighted median of the scored pool (100); the median across combinations is the priority score. Bands count buildings down the score at 1% and 10%, including a crossing record; no ranks inside bands. The uplift-off calculation is a stress test beside the result. The [building companion](issue-6-building-rating.md#3-method-contract-declared-before-the-run) is the method owner.

This is a relative priority index, not an observed resident loss share or calibrated safety probability. Known findings, missing records and the taller-building lane remain visible; legal eligibility and organisation are follow-up routes rather than danger weights. New data discovery does not silently alter this contract.

**Terms:** a *structural world* is one combination of implementation choices: Ay rule and basin corner
period kept or flattened. The *uplift-off stress test* gives C1M/RMM records the C1L/RML value of the
same era and zone; it is reported beside each result, never averaged into it, because it is a stress
scenario rather than a corrected model. A
*superseded record* shares a doorplate with a later new-build use permit, so its building may no
longer stand. *Development analysis after exposure* means the data had been seen, so a result
is never an untouched test.

**Policy weights:** distinguish scientific parameters, instrument factors, legal gates and city
preferences. Equal/PCA weights are declared comparators, not neutral safety truth. Normalization,
overlap, compensation and sensitivity require the same justification as nominal weights. Use gates
or non-compensatory rules where addition is inappropriate. W01–W03/M05 supply principles, not a
ready-made Taipei coefficient set.

**Minimal B specification / hypothesis:** on a declared project boundary, report structural/hazard
need, older-resident/rescue context and coverage, alongside a **separate** M04 suitability record
(public interest, alternatives, willingness, relocation, finance and applicable required contents).
Output: earlier suitability review / request specific evidence / defer with reason. Consent and
financing difficulties are feasibility status, not reductions in structural need. Reference M04 and
M05's area-planning purpose; M06/M07 do not supply a comparable project pool. Without comparable
projects, labelled rule scenarios can test traceability only. Reject automatic A→B transfer,
unsupported aggregation, invented approval probabilities or claimed superior selection.
**Instantiated 2026-10-21** as one research case (§5 public-renewal B): the project boundary is a
designated renewal-area piece, the need profile is A's loss-chain quantity on the piece's own records,
and the M04 record is kept for the band-1 piece with each content's evidence status.
